# Code of Conduct

We want Subjective C to be a rigorous, welcoming project where people can challenge ideas without attacking people.

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, credit others, protect private information, and avoid harassment or discriminatory behavior.

Project maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Serious concerns may be reported privately through the security contact described in `SECURITY.md`; reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
